# Ardy Support

**Last updated:** September 26, 2026

Need help with your account, your listings, or the app itself? Email us at **ardyy2026@gmail.com** and we will get back to you. Writing from the email address registered to your Ardy account helps us find it faster.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I delete my account?

Open the **Profile** tab and choose **Delete My Account**. You will be asked to confirm first. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone — it removes your account, your listings, your favorites, your buy requests, and your Land Advisor conversations. If you would rather we did it for you, see [Delete Your Ardy Account](/legal/delete-account).

### How do I contact a listing owner?

Open the listing and tap **Request to Buy**. That sends your request to the owner, who can then follow up with you directly. Everything you have sent is listed under **My Buy Requests**.

### I did not receive my password reset code.

Check your spam folder first. If nothing arrives after a few minutes, request a new code and confirm the email address matches the one on your account. Email us if it keeps failing.

## Still stuck?

Write to **ardyy2026@gmail.com** with your account email and a short description of the problem. Screenshots help.
